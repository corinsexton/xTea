Mosaic v2 validation (branch vaf-site-v2, 2026-11-13)
=====================================================

Three changes, all checked on the HapMap mixtures (BCM 300x, NYGC/UW/WashU 100x) and on the
gtval germline datasets. The write-up is in docs/supplemental/README_NOTES.md
("Per-site VAF model", "Alu-spanning pairs", "Per-sample mosaic ceiling").

A. Per-site VAF model on per-pair counts (d091488)
   tables/vaf/germline_vaf.tsv            7 germline genomes, counts at truth sites, self-calibrated
   tables/vaf/mosaic_calib_by_bin.tsv     mixtures: median VAF/truth, coverage, fraction estimated 0,
                                          by BAM, expected-VAF bin and background mode
                                          (constant = BCM value, control = measured per BAM,
                                          control_selfcal = also calibrated on the mixture's germline calls)
   tables/vaf/mosaic_calib_by_family.tsv  same by family (control background)
   tables/vaf/*_persite.tsv.gz            per-site estimates
   tables/vaf/vaf_eval.txt                full log (fits, backgrounds, HG005 sites in the mixtures)
   tables/vaf/refit_round1.txt            default-calibration refit on HG002 NovaSeq 30x
   figures/mixture_obs_vs_exp.png         estimated vs expected VAF per BAM and family
   figures/germline_vaf.png               germline het/hom VAF per genome

C. Alu-spanning pairs (tried, left out)
   tables/vaf/spanning_pair_ablation.tsv           model vs with_span vs emp_isize
   tables/vaf/germline_het_by_family_svlen.tsv     germline het VAF by family and SVLEN bin
   figures/het_vaf_vs_svlen_span_ablation.png

B. Per-sample mosaic AF_PE ceiling (91059b3)
   tables/vaf/germ_het_afpe.tsv, tables/ceiling/germ_stats.tsv   germline het AF_PE per run/family
   tables/ceiling/per_run.tsv            TP/FP/HG005/F1 per run and rule (k or ceiling fitted on all 3 runs)
   tables/ceiling/loro.tsv               leave-one-run-out
   tables/ceiling/per_run_optimum.tsv    best fixed ceiling per run
   tables/ceiling/vaf_rule_offline.tsv   VAF-model rule (reject if VAF > v_max), offline scorer
   Scoring: post-filter outputs rerun at each ceiling, one-to-one match to mix.tsv
   (+-200 bp, same family), calls at HG005 germline sites counted separately; before the
   cross-family merge.

Germline impact (gtval runs_b15, 8 datasets; genotype + gVCF rerun from the same inputs,
base 35d287f vs vaf-site-v2 with --basic-info)

   tables/germline_check/vcf_vaf_by_gt.tsv median VAF and CI width by GT, both arms

Scripts: scripts/ (paths point at /n/scratch/users/c/cos689/xtea2_review/mosaic_v2 and the
