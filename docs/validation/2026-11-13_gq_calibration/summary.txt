GQ calibration (2026-11-13, branch gq-calib)

Change: GQ = phred(eps + (1 - 2 eps) * perr), eps = GQ_ERR_FLOOR = 0.001, so GQ <= 30.
GT, GTL, rho (0.05) and the EM fit are unchanged. Inputs: B15 runs (runs_b15, 8 datasets).

Labels
  truth  : xTea-paper HG002 set (H1/H2 het, H1,H2 hom); HPRC tier1 for the 1KG samples.
  lab_q  : GIAB Q100 v5.0q HG002 GRCh38 stvar, autosomal sites inside the benchmark bed
           (q100_labels.py: per haplotype, insertion >= max(50, 0.5 SVLEN) within 200 bp);
           HPRC tier1 for the 1KG samples.
Train: HG002 NovaSeq 30x + HG00438. Held out: HiSeq 30x, 20x/15x/10x, HG02257, HG02622.

Why the errors at GQ >= 30 (39 under truth labels)
  37 are HG002, the same ~10 sites in every HG002 set (independent HiSeq library included).
  Q100 agrees with xTea's call at 35 of the 37: they are truth-label errors.
  Under Q100 labels the residual high-GQ errors are complex or nested sites (two Q100
  insertions of 849 and 1912 bp at chr22:20075431; SVLEN 75 vs 455 bp at chr18:11874557;
  duplicate calls at chr17:32310719/869; SVLEN 8 at chr13:113357040), not count noise.

Beta-binomial rho (rho_grid.tsv, joint_fit_*.tsv)
  Unsupervised marginal-likelihood MLE (rho_sr, rho_pe): NovaSeq 0.02/0.02, HiSeq 0.02/0.05,
  20x 0.01/0.02, 15x 0/0.01, 10x 0/0.005, 1KG 0.02-0.03/0.02. Not stable per sample (-> 0 at
  10x), so no per-sample fit. Labeled joint fit (Q100, train): optimum rho_sr 0.02-0.06,
  rho_pe 0.03, eps 0.0009; 0.05/0.05 is 5.1 log-lik units worse on train, 6.7 on held out.
  Larger rho cannot remove the top-bin errors: at 0.1, 5 held-out errors stay at GQ >= 30 with
  predicted error ~0 and held-out GT errors rise 84 -> 126 (446 at 0.2). Kept rho = 0.05.

eps fit (MLE on train, rho 0.05): Q100 labels 0.00084 (95% CI 0.00013-0.0025) -> 0.001.
  (truth labels give 0.0045, driven by label errors; not used.)

GT errors: unchanged in every dataset (0 GT changes; gt_errors.tsv). Totals 118 (truth), 95 (Q100).
